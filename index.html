Energy means how rarely it needs charging. Why: the charm shows the full pet and is buildable now. The ring is premium and effortless, but shows mood only as light. A screen and battery in a pendant is out of reach for a first product. The wristband loses the wrist to the watch. An app alone is never seen.

Locket

Watch out: tiny screen and battery.

Ring or band

Ring first, wristband as a variant. The pet’s mood as colour and light.

Watch out: no face, competes with watches.
